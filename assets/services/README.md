# Service icons

WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and the fallback Google Messages SVG marks are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), downloaded 2026-10-09 from `develop/icons/{whatsapp,messenger,instagram,googlemessages}.svg`. Their CC0 1.0 license is included in LICENSE.md. Only fill colors were added. Brand names and marks identify integrations; no endorsement or ownership is implied.

`googlemessages.png` is the official Google Messages icon from https://www.gstatic.com/android-messages-web/images/2022.3/2x/messages_2022_96dp.png, downloaded 2026-10-09. It is used only to identify the Google Messages integration. Google retains its trademark rights. The simple geometric mark is documented as public domain at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_Messages_icon_(2022).svg.

Icons8 was considered at the owner's suggestion. Its standard terms restrict redistribution as extractable standalone assets, so those downloads are not included in this open-source source tree.
